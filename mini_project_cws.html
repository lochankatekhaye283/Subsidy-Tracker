Facing Issues?

Trying to track down government subsidies as a regular citizen can get messy real quick.

  • Scattered Portals: Schemes sit on dozens of separate sites, so just figuring out what you qualify for takes forever.
  • No Tracking Updates: Once you apply, there's barely any clear way to tell where your DBT money is stuck.
  • Slow Issue Resolution: When a payment goes missing or is late, people end up running between offices and drowning in forms.

Why Choose Subsidy-Tracker?

Subsidy-Tracker puts your Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) and subsidy applications under one roof instead of forcing you to hop across sites.

We keep things simple: help money reach the people it's meant for without needless delays.

Consider it a straight line between government schemes and your bank account.

  • All Schemes in One Place: Check state and central options you might fit in seconds.
  • Live Application Tracking: Watch your status move from approval to bank credit.
  • Direct Support: Log complaints online when payouts stall—no agents or middlemen needed.

Types of Schemes

1. Agriculture, Rural & Environment

Farming keeps rural India moving and anchors a huge share of local livelihoods. These programs back better soil care, eco-friendly methods, and push growth across the green, white, yellow, and blue revolutions.

Explore schemes here ⌄

2. Business & Self-Employed

Small shops, freelancers, and new founders often need early cash or loans without heavy collateral. Programs like Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) and PMEGP exist to help get ideas off the ground and keep them running.

Explore schemes here ⌄

3. Health & Wellness

Working alongside state efforts and the National Health Mission (NRHM and NUHM), these schemes aim to keep basic care affordable and within reach for everyday households.

Explore schemes here ⌄

4. Driving & Transport

Drivers, fleet owners, and transport workers across Maharashtra and the rest of India can tap into schemes that help buy vehicles, replace worn-out ones, and cover welfare or medical needs.

Explore schemes here ⌄

5. Housing & Local Services

As towns and villages grow, housing and everyday basics matter most. These schemes focus on home support alongside essentials like drinking water, sanitation, and power.

Explore schemes here ⌄
